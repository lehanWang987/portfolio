Wandering SoMa




This is a small book I designed based on the article The Nerds and the Needy by Laurie Penny. At that time I wanted to rent an appartment at SoMa district and encountered many things. The article touched my very much, and I designed this book. The theme of article is about the gap between high-end technology workers and normal citizen, therefore, I make the book pages gradient from white to dark blue. The gradient is imply the change from day to night in the area and also the normal citizen to people work in front of screen.
